Build 11 - font fix and 9 recent books
- Fixed: on the X4, books could show in a blocky font with huge gaps between words (since Build 10). The firmware had grown too big for the reader to load a whole font next to it; it now loads the text sizes it needs one at a time, so Bookerly, Alegreya, Literata and Atkinson Hyperlegible all work again. If a font ever can't load, the text is now drawn plainly instead of with gaps.
- The cover home page shows your 9 most recent books (three rows of three) instead of 3.
- GitHub's build now checks that the fonts fit next to the X4 firmware, so this can't slip out again.
